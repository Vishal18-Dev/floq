Privacy policy

Effective from 24 November 2026 · Last updated 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information the floq app and website collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. floq is run by Vishal Rao, an individual based in Bengaluru, India, who runs floq ("floq", "we", "us").

floq is used by shop owners and their staff ("merchants"). When a merchant records a sale or a credit entry, they may also enter details about their own customers. For that customer data, the merchant decides what to record and floq stores it on the merchant's behalf.

1. Information we collect

From merchants and staff

  • Account details: mobile number, name, and optionally an email address.
  • Shop details: shop name (in English and your language), type of business, address, opening hours, GSTIN if you add one, and your UPI ID.
  • Security details: your app PIN, which we store only as a one-way hash. Fingerprint and face unlock happen on your phone; we never receive your fingerprint or face data.
  • Staff: names, mobile numbers and roles of people you invite.
  • Business records: products, prices, stock, suppliers, orders, bills, payments, dues and daily totals.
  • Subscription payments: the plan you choose and the UPI reference you submit. We do not collect card or bank account numbers.

About a merchant's customers

  • If a merchant chooses to record them: a customer's name, mobile number, what they bought and any amount they owe.

Collected automatically

  • Activity logs: a record of key actions (for example, a refund or price change), who did them and when, to protect the shop against mistakes and misuse.
  • Technical data: app version, device type, network status and error reports, used to keep the app working.
  • Website: our website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.

2. How we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, show your catalogue, create bills and tokens, sync sales between devices, and show daily totals.
  • To keep accounts secure, including detecting repeated wrong PINs or OTPs.
  • To manage your subscription and confirm payments.
  • To give support when you contact us, and to send service messages about your account.
  • To fix problems and improve floq, using aggregated or de-identified data where we can.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use your business or customer data for advertising.

3. Payments

floq shows a UPI QR code built from your own UPI ID. Your customers pay you directly through their UPI app and bank. floq does not receive, hold or process that money.

When you tap to send a WhatsApp reminder or bill, floq opens WhatsApp on your phone with the message filled in. The message is then sent by you through WhatsApp, under WhatsApp's own terms.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. If you close your account, we delete or anonymise it within [90] days, except records we must keep longer by law (for example, tax and accounting records). Our database backups roll over automatically and are deleted on their normal cycle.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). PINs are hashed, each shop's data is kept separate from every other shop's, and staff see only what their role allows. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your PIN private and tell us at once if you think your account has been misused.

7. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it,
  • correct or update it,
  • delete it, or withdraw consent you gave, where the law allows,
  • nominate someone to act for you if you are unable to.

You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings, then Delete account) or as described at getfloq.in/delete-account. Merchants can edit or delete most other data in the app. For anything else, write to us at the address below. If you are a shop's customer, please contact that shop first, as they control the record; we will help them respond.

8. Children

floq is a business tool for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we will tell you in the app or by SMS or WhatsApp before they take effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
