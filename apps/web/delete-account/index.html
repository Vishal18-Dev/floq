Delete your floq account

floq (the "floq Merchant" app by Vishal Rao) lets you delete your account and its data at any time.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open floq and tap your shop name at the top to open Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE and tap Delete permanently.

Deletion happens straight away and cannot be undone.

No longer have the app?

Email privacy@floqapp.in from any address, with the subject "Delete my floq account" and the mobile number you signed up with. We will confirm that the number is yours by SMS before deleting anything, and complete the request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • If you are the shop owner: your login, your shop and everything in it, including items, prices, stock, bills, payments, credit customers, dues, daily totals, settings and activity logs. Every staff login on your shop is deleted too.
  • If you are staff or a manager: only your own login. The shop and its records belong to the owner and stay with them.

What we keep

Records we must keep by law, such as invoices for your floq subscription, are kept in our accounts for as long as the law requires. Copies in our encrypted database backups are overwritten on their normal cycle, within [30] days. Bills or messages you already sent to customers through WhatsApp or SMS are outside floq and are not affected.

Questions? See our privacy policy or write to privacy@floqapp.in.
