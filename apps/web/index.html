FAQ

Questions, answered

Still unsure? WhatsApp us and we will set it up with you.

Do I need internet all the time?

No. Cash sales work offline and sync when the network is back. UPI needs the customer's phone to be online.

Which phones does floq work on?

Any Android phone or tablet. Get it from Google Play or Indus Appstore.

Does my money go through floq?

Never. The QR uses your own UPI ID, so payments go straight to your bank.

Can my staff use it?

Yes. Invite helpers with a code. Each person opens floq with their own fingerprint or PIN.

What happens after the 7 free days?

Pick monthly or yearly and pay by UPI inside the app. Your bills and stock stay safe either way.

Can you set it up for me?

Yes, for free. Send us your menu or product list on WhatsApp and we add it for you.
